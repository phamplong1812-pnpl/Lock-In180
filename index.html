Today's LOCK IN mission

Day 1 · Reset the business direction around proof and a second acquisition channel.

Mission: Define your E-commerce Video Ads offer

90 MIN CORE
You have 5–10 hours per week, not two hours every day. Today's plan is intentionally 90 minutes. If it's late or you're busy, do the 30-minute minimum and continue another day.

Block 1 · Learn one skill · 20 min

Study the structure of a direct-response product video: hook → product problem/desire → demonstration → benefit → call to action. Analyze two ads and write down the structure. Do not just watch passively.

Block 2 · Produce an asset · 45 min

Create a 15–20 second vertical sample using footage you own or have permission to use. Focus on one product, one benefit, readable captions and a clear opening. Export a draft and save it as Case-01-Draft.mp4.

Block 3 · Market research · 25 min

Review five small e-commerce brands' public social content. Note product category, current video style, one improvement opportunity and why your sample would be relevant. Do not send unsolicited messages to strangers today.

30-minute minimum

10 min analyze one ad · 15 min edit a 5–8 second hook · 5 min record one brand profile and one improvement opportunity.
